Tech Services

Websites, automation, and practical tech help for small teams and businesses that want things to work better without making them more complicated.

Overview

I know a lot of small teams don’t need another platform or a giant tech overhaul. They usually need someone to look at what they’re already using, figure out where things are getting stuck, and teach them how to use it or make it work better.

A few of the ways I can help

  • Build or improve websites that are clean, accessible, and easy for you to update yourself.
  • Automate repetitive work by connecting forms, spreadsheets, email, and the tools you already use.
  • Organize and clean up data so it’s easier to use, understand, and report on.
  • Help you choose technology that actually fits what you need without paying for a bunch of features you’ll never use.

A good fit if…

You’re a small team, nonprofit, school, or solo business owner spending too much time on manual, repetitive work and you want someone who can understand the problem, build something practical, explain how it works, and leave you with a system you can actually use.
