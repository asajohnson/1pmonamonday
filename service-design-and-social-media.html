Design & Social Media

Design, branding, graphics, and social media support that helps your work look clear, professional, and connect with the people you're trying to reach.

Overview

You don't need a big marketing budget or a full-time designer to have a consistent, professional look.

I help businesses and organizations create graphics, promotional materials, and social media content that looks good and gets the message across.

Whether you're starting from scratch or just need to make everything a little more consistent, I can help you put together something that works and is easy for you to maintain.

A few of the ways I can help

  • Create logos, color schemes, and basic branding that gives your organization a consistent look.
  • Design flyers, presentations, signage, event materials, and other graphics.
  • Set up or improve social media accounts and figure out what content makes sense for your audience.
  • Create social media graphics, templates, and content calendars to make posting easier.
  • Refresh existing materials to make them clearer, more consistent, and more professional.

A good fit if…

You're doing work you're proud of, but the look of it is inconsistent — or posting keeps falling to the bottom of the list.

You need some design or social media help but doesn't have the budget or need for a full-time designer.

You want someone who can take your ideas, make them look good, and create materials you can easily use and update yourself.
