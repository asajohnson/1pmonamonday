Product & Program Development

From kickoff to close-out — planning, coordination, and delivery for programs and products that actually ship, on time and in scope.

Overview

Coming up with a good idea is usually the easy part. Turning it into something real takes planning, coordination, problem-solving, and a lot of follow-through.

I've spent years managing products and programs, from large technology companies to schools and nonprofits. I know how to take a complicated project, break it into manageable pieces, get the right people involved, and keep things moving when priorities change or unexpected problems come up.

Whether you're starting something new or trying to get an existing project back on track, I can help figure out what needs to happen and make sure it gets done.

A few of the ways I can help

  • Turn an idea into a realistic project plan with clear goals, responsibilities, milestones, and timelines.
  • Coordinate people and teams, keep communication organized, and make sure everyone knows what's happening.
  • Manage budgets, resources, risks, and all the little details that can derail a project.
  • Identify problems early, work through roadblocks, and adjust plans when things don't go as expected.
  • Help launch or wrap up projects, document what worked, and make the next one easier.

A good fit if…

You've got a project, product, or program that needs to get off the ground (or across the finish line), but you don't have the time, resources, or dedicated person to manage all the moving pieces.

You need someone who can see the big picture, handle the details, work with different kinds of people, and actually get things done.
